Smashy stock icons, one folder per game, resized to 64x64 webp. The default costume is <game>/<key>.webp;
the other costumes (round 728) are <game>/costumes/<key>-<n>.webp, from the same sources (not Smash 64).

ultimate/  Recreated by Max_Hamm (reddit.com/user/Max_Hamm, r/smashbros): "People can use them however they
           want, no credit required." Ridley uses costume 5 (the default wasn't in the set).
smash64/   The Spriters Resource, ripped by Storice (Pikachu/Jigglypuff extras by Poke-Brother).
melee/     The Spriters Resource, ripped by Cotton Candy 2C.
brawl/     The Spriters Resource, ripped by Cotton Candy 2C.
smash4/    The Spriters Resource, ripped by Random Talking Bush.

Characters and icon designs (c) Nintendo / HAL Laboratory. Smashy isn't affiliated with Nintendo.
To stop using them: set STOCK_ICONS = false in src/lib/mainBadge.ts (badges fall back to text codes).
Re-import: scripts/import-stock-icons.mjs (Ultimate) and scripts/import-sheet-stock-icons.mjs (the rest),
then scripts/import-stock-costumes.mjs for the other costumes.
